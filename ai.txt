# Easter - PropFirmDiscount.com

Easter prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

25 deals from 20 prop firms, newest 2026-04-07.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
